# LORE DESIGN — SCIW Lorebook Plan

---

## 1. Rank ladder (authoritative)

Ranks are Guild-issued legal standing, separate from Level. **Geometric, not additive.**

| Rank | Title | AV band | Solos | Standing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | Witnessed | 0 – 499 | nothing dangerous | citizen |
| D | Measured | 500 – 1,999 | Grade I | licensed |
| C | Attested | 2,000 – 7,999 | Grade II | full standing, may lead |
| B | Wielded | 8,000 – 31,999 | Grade III | officer class |
| A | Sovereign | 32,000 – 127,999 | Grade IV | may refuse a noble summons |
| S | Named | 128,000 – 511,999 | Grade V | Crown treats as peer |
| SS | Unwritten | 512,000 – 2,047,999 | Grade VI | no legal standing |
| SSS | Anomalous | 2,048,000+ | unknown | Guild has no procedure |

**Above SSS: TRANSCENDENT.** Not a rank. A category the world has no slot for. The Guild does not
publish it; the Church does not recognise it. Two confirmed in living memory, both unverified.

The four gates (AV threshold, skill attestation, registered deed, the Trial) mirror the standard
progression-fantasy structure, but the **Trial** cannot be waived and only clears Ranks D through A.

**Anti-ladder rule:** rank is a ceiling, not an outcome. Never narrate a lower-ranked character
simply losing by default.

## 2. Entry template (used for all eight rank entries)

```
DEFINITION      — one sentence, in-world
SOLO CEILING    — what of this rank can reliably be put down
STANDING        — legal and civic position
REQUIREMENTS    — the gates, concretely
CEILING         — what stops them going higher
EXEMPLAR        — a named, dated, canonical individual
```

The **CEILING** field is what makes each rank substantive rather than a bigger number.

## 3. Lorebook activation plan

Target: **~75 entries**.

| Group | # | Activation |
|---|---|---|
| SYSTEM | 10 | status block, notifications, level, stats, classes, skills, unique skills, titles, status effects, the anomaly |
| RANKS | 9 | ladder + eight ranks |
| RACES | 11 | human, elf x3, beastfolk, demonkin, giant, spirit, dragon-kin, undead, celestial |
| MAGIC | 8 | elements, divine, demonology, necromancy, summoning, alchemy, enchanting, runes |
| CLASSES | 8 | system, basic, advanced, unique, Unclassed, evolvers, crafting, tamers |
| GEOGRAPHY | 10 | world, kingdom, empire, demon continent, beast plains, archipelago, frozen north, elf forest, Dead Lands |
| DUNGEONS | 6 | Rift, grades, floors, bosses, labyrinth events, skill stones |
| MONSTERS | 6 | tiers, common, elite, boss, named, apex |
| FACTIONS | 8 | Guild, Church, Nobles, Demon Court, Merchants, Academy, Mercenaries, Slavers |
| CULTURE | 7 | society, economy, pantheon, eras, adventurer system, half-bloods, magic industry |
| ISEKAI | 6 | otherworlders, transmigration, summoning, reincarnation, return protocol, anti-isekai |
| ITEMS | 5 | weapon tiers, armour, legendary, cursed, skill stones |
| RULES | 3 | power scaling, rank vs outcome, transcendent |

**Constant entries: 3 only** — the status-block format, the power-scaling rule, and the notification
format. Everything else keyword-gated.

**Keyword hygiene:** keys are `rank e`, `dungeon grade iii`, `beastfolk` — never bare `magic` or
`world`, which would fire constantly.

## 4. Anti-contradiction checklist

- [ ] All 8 ranks present exactly once, bands strictly ascending.
- [ ] Transcendent is described as a category, not a rank with a band.
- [ ] Ryl (Level 14, Rank E, Unclassed, PER 18) satisfies every constraint the entries state.
- [ ] Level 14 ≥ the Rank E minimum of 10.
- [ ] [REGISTRY] limitations stated identically in the anomaly entry and the card.
- [ ] Ryl never described as licensed to the Guild (he is Unclassed, which forbids it).
- [ ] Every class named in the world draft appears in the class entries.
- [ ] Monster grades align with dungeon grades.
- [ ] Every race in the world draft has an entry.
