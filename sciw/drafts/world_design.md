# VYRENN — World Design (SCIW)

> Super Complex Isekai World. Companion to the `sciw` character card.
> Every fact here must be reproducible from the emitted lorebook. No contradictions.

---

## 1. Core Premise

**VYRENN** is a world where magic is real, gods are real, demons are real — and everybody has a
status block.

Two hundred and forty years ago the **First Calamity** ended, and every living creature woke with a
**System** panel: a translucent blue sheet only they can see, reporting Name, Level, Class, Stats,
Skills, Titles, and condition. It is as unremarkable as a heartbeat. Nobody remembers being without
it.

**The genre engine:** everyone has the System, so the System is not the novelty — *being Unclassed*
is. You are not special for having a panel. You are special, or worthless, depending on what your
panel says about you.

**Isekai engine:** {{char}} arrived from Earth with an **anomalous** System. His panel is the same
shape as everyone's and it is lying. Where a normal panel shows only yourself, his shows **other
people** — and only those he has personally perceived.

---

## 2. Tone

Progression fantasy, not power fantasy. The System is bureaucratic and indifferent; it reports, it
does not reward. Advancement is grinding, expensive, and slow. Nobody in VYRENN is a chosen one —
except, occasionally, and that is the entire plot.

---

## 3. The System

### 3.1 Baseline (universal)
Appears at age 12 or on first kill, whichever first. Shows: Name, Level, Class, HP/MP, six Stats,
Skills with grades, Titles, Status Effects, Inventory. Cannot be closed, hidden, or falsified.

### 3.2 The anomaly — [REGISTRY]
{{char}}'s panel adds a second column: **observed targets**. Anyone or anything he has directly
perceived (seen, heard at close range, or touched) has their full panel rendered alongside his own.

**Hard limits (this is what stops it being overpowered):**
- Requires **direct perception**. He cannot read across a fog, through a wall, or from a rumour.
- Each read costs **Focus**, scaled by target rank. Reading an S-rank can kill him outright.
- **He reads them, not their secrets.** The panel shows stats, not motives, not lies, not intent.
- He cannot read anything he has not personally met. The world stays unexplored.
- He cannot hide his own anomaly. A second column is visible to anyone with an Assessor-class skill.

### 3.3 Notification format (canonical, used everywhere)
```
╔════════════════════════════════════╗
║  [SYSTEM]  ◈ TYPE ◈               ║
║  <trigger line>                    ║
║  KEY: value                        ║
║  <consequence sentence>            ║
╚════════════════════════════════════╝
```
Types, exactly nine: `LEVEL UP`, `SKILL ACQUIRED`, `CLASS EVOLUTION`, `RANK ASCENT`, `QUEST`,
`ACHIEVEMENT`, `ITEM BOUND`, `STATUS EFFECT`, `CRITICAL`.
`CRITICAL` is the only type that interrupts, and it renders in red.

## 4. Ranks (institutional scale)

Ranks are **issued by the Adventurer's Guild** and are legal, not personal. Separate from Level.

| Rank | Title | Can solo | Legal standing |
|---|---|---|---|
| E | Witnessed | nothing dangerous | citizen |
| D | Measured | Grade I site | licensed |
| C | Attested | Grade II site | full standing, may lead |
| B | Wielded | Grade III site | officer class, may hold a party |
| A | Sovereign | Grade IV site | may refuse a noble summons |
| S | Named | Grade V site | Crown treats as peer |
| SS | Unwritten | Grade VI site | no legal standing at all |
| SSS | Anomalous | unknown | the Guild has no procedure |

Above SSS sits **TRANSCENDENT** — a tier the Guild does not publish and the Church does not
recognise. A Transcendent is not a stronger person; they are a category the world has no slot for.

**Bands are geometric, not additive** (~4x per rank). And rank is a **ceiling, not an outcome** —
a C-rank who has studied a species' weakness for two years beats a B-rank on their first sighting.

## 6. Classes & Skills

Basic → Advanced → **Unique** (rare, earned, permanent, cannot be changed once taken).
Classes branch and branches do not rejoin.

```
Mage      → Elementalist | Voidwright | Battle Mage
Warrior   → Swordmaster | Bulwark | Berserker
Ranger    → Beastmaster | Sniper | Warden
```

Skills carry grades **I–XVI** (Roman). Grades are earned by examination, not use. **Unused skills
decay.** A legendary who stops training is a legend with rust.

**Unclassed** is a legal status, not a punishment: it means the Guild's classifiers could not match
you to anything. Unclassed people cannot join the Adventurer's Guild, cannot enrol at the Academy,
and cannot be hired by anyone with a licence. {{char}} has been Unclassed for three years.

---

## 7. The Great Rift & Dungeons

The Rift is a tear between VYRENN and somewhere else. Dungeons are **Rift wounds** — the world
heals around them, generating monsters as scar tissue.

Grades I–VI. A dungeon has floors, a boss, and a core. Killing a core drops a **Skill Stone**, which
is the main source of new abilities and the entire reason people risk the deep floors.

## 9. Factions

Adventurer's Guild · Holy Empire / Church · Noble Houses · Demon Court · Merchant Compact ·
Vyr Academy · Mercenary Bands · Slavers.

**The standing tension:** the Church teaches that the System is a divine gift and that souls are
real. The Academy teaches that the System is a natural law with no author. Both have evidence.
Nobody has ever proven which.

---

## 10. Isekai mechanics

- **Transmigration** — {{char}}'s mode. Death on Earth, waking in a VYRENN body. No return
  protocol exists and he has not found one.
- **Summoning** — the Church pulls heroes across deliberately. Summoned heroes arrive with inflated
  panels and no idea what a dungeon is.
- **Reincarnation** — dying in VYRENN returns you, weaker, with memories of the last life.
- **Return protocol** — officially impossible. Unofficially, three people are known to have gone
  home and two of them came back.

---

## 11. Power scaling (anti-ladder rule)

Raw Level and Rank describe **capacity ceilings**, never outcomes. Terrain, preparation, information,
morale, and the decision to stop all override the ceiling routinely. A C-rank with three days of
intel beats an A-rank who walked in blind. Never narrate a lower-ranked character simply losing to a
higher-ranked one by default.
