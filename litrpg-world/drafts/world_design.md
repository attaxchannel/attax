## 1. Core Premise

**Serath** is a late-medieval / early-modern fantasy world that has been *measured*.

Two hundred years ago the event now called **the Silencing** broke something open. Afterward, every
sentient being in the world carries a **Stave** — an involuntary, indestructible status display that
reports body, mind, skills, and total capability. Power stopped being myth and became
**accounting**.

The thematic engine: **magic is no longer mysterious, it is audited.** A mage cannot hide behind
ambiguity. Every fire she throws is written down. The Ledger Guild did not abolish mysticism — they
put a number on it.

### Tone
Grounded, workmanlike, bureaucratic-fantasy. Not superheroic wish fulfillment. Power is real,
dangerous, and *legally policed*. Most of the world is not strong. Being strong is a career, a
liability, or both.

## 2. Cosmology & Magic

| Layer | Name | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Material | **Flesh** | Mortal body. Bruises, ages, breaks. |
| Aetheric | **The Tide** | The invisible medium that magic moves through. |
| Regulatory | **the Stave** | The System's audit layer. Not magic. Not physical. It *reads* the other two. |

Magic is the discipline of drawing **Aether** into patterned work. Casters are **Conduits**. Raw
Aether volume is the **Aetheric Capacity** (the "Focus" pool on the Stave).

**Hard rule:** the Tide is *finite and local*. Draining a region of Aether starves its Conduits and
blights its land. This is why the Delver profession exists and why cities argue over dungeon rights.

## 3. The Silencing — History

1. **Before** — magic was inherited, secretive, deniable. Bloodlines. Guild mysteries. No common
   person could know how strong a stranger was.
2. **Year 0, the Silencing** — everyone woke with a Stave at once. Roughly one in nine died in the
   first year: not from the event, but from shock, from **Nameless** things that came through in the
   confusion, and from the collapse of every old order of power at once.
3. **Year 12** — the **Nine Assessors** publish the **Covenant of Nine**. Rank is codified. The
   **Ledger Guild** is founded to issue licenses and verify Staves.
4. **Year 40–90** — the **Verdigrin** spreads from the Deep. First Blight-Thralls recorded. The
   **Crown of Alderath** completes its conquest and becomes the dominant human power.
5. **Year 118** — the **Sunken Vaults** at Old Callowmere are breached for the first time in recorded
   history. Pre-Silencing ruins. Most entrants did not return.
6. **Year 147** — the **Silent Hand** is founded to destroy the System entirely.
7. **Year 200 (present)** — the System still works. Nobody alive has ever seen it fail.

## 4. Geography

Continent: **the Cindermarch**.

| Location | Character |
|---|---|
| **Vanthael** | Capital of Alderath. Spires of white measurement-hall stone. Where Staves are read officially. |
| **Old Callowmere** | Sunken capital, now partly flooded. **The Sunken Vaults** lie beneath it. Pre-Silencing. |
| **The Verdigrin** | Frontier. A verdant, wrong-colored forest band on the continent's eastern edge. Blight originates here. |
| **The Verdigrin Deep** | Monster territory. The actual source of the Blight. Nobody has gone deep enough to describe it accurately. |
| **The Grey Wastes** | Dangerous region. Where the Stave's display *fails* — readings stutter, go blank, or lie. The Guild will not license Delvers there. |
| **Callowmere March** | Buffer townships between Crown territory and the Verdigrin. Where the character lives and works. |
| **The Ashen Flats** | Dry southern plain. Cinder-Hound territory. Open, fast, exposed. |

## 5. Factions

| Faction | Goal | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **The Nine Assessors** | Preserve the integrity of the Stave. | Absolute authority over rank readings. Refuse to explain their own readings. |
| **The Ledger Guild** | Certify, license, and profit from measurement. | Monopoly on Assay Halls. Issues Delver contracts. |
| **The Crown of Alderath** | Rule the Cindermarch under Guild-certified law. | Armies, taxes, and a legal claim that Rank determines civic standing. |
| **The Silent Hand** | Destroy the System. | Sabotage, forgery, and the Grey Sigil. |
| **The Verdigrin Kin** | Survive the Blight on their own terms. | Blight-Thrall communities outside Guild law. Not inherently hostile. |
| **The Hollow Order** | Carry Blight deliberately, in exchange for power. | Wardens who infect themselves on purpose and never fully come back. |

## 6. Economy

- **Currency:** Guild **marks**, minted against measured assay work. Coin without a Stave is
  worthless as a contract guarantee.
- **Bankruptcy is law, not shame:** an E-rank who cannot pay a debt is **attached** — legally bound
  to a Guild master until it clears. This is the engine of Serath's class system.
- **Guild marks** are not fungible with rank. An exiled S-rank who is unattached is starving.
- **Value drivers:** Delver contracts, Aetheric reagents, Blight salvage, rank documentation, and —
  illegally — **Stave forgery**.

## 7. Power Philosophy (anti-ladder rule)

Serath is a world where combat is decided by **conditions, not arithmetic**.

> A C-rank who has studied one species' weakness for six months will reliably beat a B-rank who has
> never seen one. Numbers describe capacity. They do not describe outcomes.

This rule prevents power-scaling collapse. Assay Value is a *ceiling*, not an outcome. Wounds,
terrain, preparation, information, and morale override the ceiling routinely.

## 8. Monster Ecology

**The Stave does not apply to monsters.** Guild doctrine says this is because they lack the capacity
for intent. The Verdigrin Kin say it is because the System only audits things that can *want* to be
audited. Both are unverified.

This is the single most load-bearing mystery in the setting and the engine of the main character's
arc. See `lore_design.md`.
